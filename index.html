FAQ

How long do websites take?

E-commerce stores generally take 7–14 days. Urgent e-commerce projects can be completed in 5 days where the project requirements allow it. Advanced websites take around 24 hours, while simple websites can be completed in around 12 hours.

What are targeted ads?

Targeted ads are online advertisements shown to people who are more likely to be interested in your product or service. Instead of showing your advert to everyone, we help focus your advertising on the right audience based on factors such as location, interests and behaviour.

Do you offer ongoing website management?
